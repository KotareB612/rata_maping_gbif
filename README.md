#### Map showing reports of each climbing rātā species, alongside observations of myrtle rust from iNaturalist and the predicted risk of myrtle rust based on climate. The rātā speceis are all in the *Metrosideros* genus: *M. albiflora,M. diffusa, M. carminea, M. colensoi M. fulgens, M. perforata*

**You can:**
* Use the side bar to click on different species and see where they have been found.
* Search your location in the left-hand box.

Please note that the daily risk of myrtle rust risk shown on the map is an annual average and does not consider species differences vulnerability or seasonal infection patterns. Also, the reports of myrtle rust are for all host species. For further information on seasonal and daily infection risk, and species vulnerability, please see there myrtle rust prediction website [here](https://myrtlerust.com/disease/plant/niwa-maps) 
   
  
    
      
**Data used**   
The occurrence data was downloaded from [GBIF](https://www.gbif.org/), an international open source data base which includes most published data on species occurrence and research grade data from iNaturalist.   
  
Reported observation of Myrtle rust from iNaturlist on the 21/08/2008. Data was filtered to only include research grade reports, which are those verified by a member of the iNaturlist community.
    
Myrtle rust mean infection risk layer was downloaded from the Landcare research website, available [here] (https://datastore.landcareresearch.co.nz/dataset/myrtaceae-sdms-and-areas-of-myrtle-rust-refugia) and is published in a paper by James McCarthy.
